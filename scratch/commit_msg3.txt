fix: correct multivariate genome-wide formula term order

The correct ASReml-R formula for a multi-environment model is:
  ~ diag(Trait):vm(merge.by, covObj)  (composite additive G x E)
  + diag(Trait):merge.by             (residual polygenic G x E)

NOT the previous (incorrect) suffix order:
  ~ vm(merge.by, covObj):diag(Trait) + merge.by
